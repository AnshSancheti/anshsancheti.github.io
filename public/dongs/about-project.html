DONGs Gallery

A curated, searchable index of 1,044 websites featured in VSauce's DONGs series, including many destinations altered or lost to time.

Canonical URL: https://anshsancheti.github.io/dongs/gallery.html Creator: Ansh Sancheti Status: Public web archive and gallery

What it is

DONGs Gallery collects 1,044 websites, games, tools, experiments, and curiosities featured in VSauce's DONGs videos and original Blogspot archive. Each entry includes its original name and destination, a screenshot, a concise description, descriptive tags, and a zero-to-three-star editorial rating.

The project is a browsable record of a particular era of the web. Many original destinations have changed, expired, become unsafe, or depended on discontinued technology such as Flash. Where appropriate, the gallery substitutes an Internet Archive copy for a lost original.

This is an independent index created by Ansh Sancheti, not an official VSauce project. Featured names, videos, websites, and linked works belong to their respective creators.

What visitors can do

  • Search all 1,044 entries by name and description.
  • Filter by editorial rating and descriptive tags.
  • Save bookmarks locally in the browser.
  • Open a random destination.
  • Follow the original playlist and Blogspot archive.
